Threads 工具隱私說明Threads Tool Privacy Notice

生效日：2026-10-29Effective Date: 2026-10-29

簡短版：這頁說明的是 Pawnch 團隊經營 Threads 帳號用的內部工具，不是 Pawnch iOS app（app 的隱私政策在這裡）。這個工具會讀取我們自己帳號的資料，以及用關鍵字搜尋到的 Threads 公開貼文。搜尋結果只存在團隊的電腦上，30 天後自動刪除，不分享、不販售。

TL;DR: This notice covers the internal tool the Pawnch team uses to run our Threads account. It is not about the Pawnch iOS app (see the app privacy policy). The tool reads our own account's data and public Threads posts found by keyword search. Search results stay on our team's computer, are deleted automatically after 30 days, and are never shared or sold.

這個工具是什麼？What is this tool?

Pawnch 有一個 Threads 帳號，由吉祥物 Miso 發文。我們用一個只在團隊電腦上執行的工具來排程貼文、看成效，並找出聊拳擊、跳繩、深夜運動的公開貼文，讓 Miso 偶爾回覆。這個工具只有我們團隊自己在用，沒有其他人能登入。

Pawnch runs a Threads account voiced by our mascot, Miso. We use a tool that runs only on our team's computer to schedule posts, review performance, and find public posts about boxing, jump rope, or late-night workouts so Miso can occasionally reply. Only our team uses this tool; no one else can sign in to it.

會讀取哪些資料？What data does it read?

  • 我們自己 Threads 帳號的貼文、回覆和成效數據。
  • 用關鍵字（例如「拳擊」「跳繩」）搜尋到的 Threads 公開貼文：作者帳號名稱、貼文內容、發文時間、貼文連結和貼文 ID。
  • Posts, replies, and performance data from our own Threads account.
  • Public Threads posts found by keyword search (for example "boxing" or "jump rope"): the author's username, the post text, the posting time, the post link, and the post ID.

我們不會讀取私人訊息、追蹤名單、個人檔案細節，也不會讀取非公開的貼文。

We do not read private messages, follower lists, profile details, or any post that is not public.

用在哪裡？How do we use it?

搜尋結果只用來決定 Miso 要不要回覆某則貼文，以及避免重複回覆同一則。每則回覆都由團隊成員手動挑選、修改、確認後才發布，每天最多 3 則。工具不會自動按讚、追蹤、轉發或回覆。

Search results are used only to decide whether Miso should reply to a post, and to avoid replying to the same post twice. Every reply is picked, edited, and confirmed by a team member before it is published, and we publish at most three replies a day. The tool never likes, follows, reposts, or replies automatically.

我們不會分享或販售這些資料，也不會用來投放廣告或建立個人檔案。

We do not share or sell this data, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

存在哪裡、存多久？Where is it stored, and for how long?

搜尋結果只存在團隊的電腦上，不會上傳到我們的伺服器（我們沒有伺服器）。搜尋結果在 30 天後自動刪除。

Search results are stored only on our team's computer. They are not uploaded to any server of ours (we do not run one). Search results are deleted automatically after 30 days.

Miso 實際發布過的回覆，我們會保留一份紀錄：我們的回覆內容、被回覆貼文的連結、ID 和作者帳號名稱，用來避免重複回覆。這些回覆本來就公開在 Threads 上。

For replies Miso actually published, we keep a record of our reply text and the link, ID, and author username of the post we replied to, so we never reply to the same post twice. These replies are already public on Threads.

怎麼申請刪除資料？How to request deletion

如果你希望我們刪除跟你的 Threads 帳號有關的紀錄，請寫信到 yi@honulab.com，附上你的 Threads 帳號名稱。我們會刪除工具裡所有跟這個帳號有關的搜尋結果和回覆紀錄，並在 30 天內回信確認。如果你也希望我們刪除 Miso 在 Threads 上給你的回覆，請在信裡說明。

If you want us to delete records related to your Threads account, email yi@honulab.com with your Threads username. We will delete all search results and reply records related to that account from our tool and confirm by email within 30 days. If you also want us to delete Miso's reply to you on Threads, just say so in your email.

本說明的異動Changes to this notice

若這份說明有任何更新，我們會直接更新這個頁面，並更新上方的生效日期。

If this notice is ever updated, we will revise this page directly and update the effective date above.

有任何隱私相關問題，歡迎寫信給我們：yi@honulab.com

Questions about privacy? Contact us at yi@honulab.com
